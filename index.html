Q-ASSIST: QuAlitative reSearch Supporting IncluSive Trials

Despite growing attention to equity and diversity (EDI) in research, clinical trials often struggle to include participants from underserved or underrepresented communities. This lack of inclusion limits the generalisability of findings and may add to health inequities.

Qualitative research, which focuses on understanding people’s experiences, the context in which experiences occur, and participant perspectives, has increasingly been recognized as a powerful tool for supporting inclusive research design and evaluation. However, there is little structured guidance on how qualitative methods can be systematically applied to support EDI in clinical trials.

The QuAlitative reSearch Supporting IncluSive Trials (Q-ASSIST study) was developed to address this gap by exploring how qualitative methods can support inclusivity across all stages of a clinical trial. The study examines how qualitative research can be better integrated across the trial lifecycle to address these inclusion gaps.
