The Q-ASSIST team

  • Dr. Linda Biesty

    Deputy Director Evidence Synthesis Ireland & Cochrane Ireland

    Dr Linda Biesty is an Associate Professor in Midwifery at University of Galway, a midwife and qualitative researcher with over 20 years of experience weaving research into clinical practice across a range of health and social questions. She is Deputy Director of Evidence Synthesis Ireland, a Research Advisor with the Qualitative Research in Trials Centre (QUESTS), and a member of the Executive Management Committee of the HRB-Trials Methodology Research Network (HRB-TMRN). Her qualitative-in-trials methodology work spans the Encounter Study, the MERIT Project, and QUALShare, alongside qualitative evidence syntheses focused on trial recruitment and retention; all exploring how qualitative research can make trials more rigorous, relevant, and inclusive. That experience is exactly what she brings to leading Q-ASSIST.

  • Dr. Catherine Houghton

    Associate Professor, School of Nursing, Midwifery and Evidence Science, University of Galway

    Catherine Houghton is Associate Professor in the School of Nursing, Midwifery and Evidence Science at the University of Galway. Catherine is a qualitative researcher with research focusing primarily qualitative evidence synthesis methodology, psychosocial research in dementia care and the integration of qualitative research within trials. Catherine is a research associate with Health Research Board-Trials Methodology Research Network (HRB-TMRN) and Evidence Synthesis Ireland supporting the development of rigorous, evidence-informed health research. Catherine is also a co-convenor of the Cochrane Qualitative Implementation Methods group.

  • Dr. Laura Jones

    Professor of Qualitative and Mixed Methods Women’s Health Research, University of Birmingham

    Laura Jones (she/her) is Professor of Qualitative and Mixed Methods Women’s Health Research at the University of Birmingham. Her research uses qualitative and mixed methods to improve the design, delivery, and evaluation of inclusive health interventions and clinical trials, particularly in women’s and maternal health. Within Q-ASSIST, Laura brings expertise in qualitative research, process evaluation, patient and stakeholder engagement, and inclusive trials methodology. Her work focuses on understanding and addressing barriers to participation for underserved groups, supporting meaningful involvement, and learning how trial processes can be adapted in practice to make research more accessible, relevant, and equitable.
